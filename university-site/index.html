About

Universidad Waraira is a fictional demo institution. It exists so that ScholarKey can show how a university website hosts its own public key. It does not describe a real campus, faculty or graduates.

Digital credentials

Diplomas from Universidad Waraira are issued as verifiable digital credentials. Anyone can check one at ScholarKey. The university publishes its public signing key at /.well-known/did.json.
